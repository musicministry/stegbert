Hymn Schedules

November 30, 2025 (First Sunday in Advent) through January 11, 2026 (Baptism of the Lord)

All hymns are taken from the blue Gather hymnal unless otherwise noted. “R&A” indicates Respond and Acclaim. Christmas season Mass parts will be provided as handouts. Click on any title to listen to a recording for rehearsal purposes, but note that the lyrics may not match our hymnal. Please practice the lyrics in the Gather hymnal, regardless of the video.

ImportantTake heed!

The Mass setting changes for Advent, the Solemnity of the Immaculate Conception, and Christmas.

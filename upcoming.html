Hymn Schedules

November 8, 2026 (Thirty-Second Sunday in Ordinary Time) through January 10, 2027 (Baptism of the Lord)

All hymns are taken from the blue Gather hymnal unless otherwise noted. “R&A” indicates Respond and Acclaim. Please note that the Mass setting is indicated for every week at the top of each list. Click its name to jump to links for Mass parts. Click on any title to listen to a recording for rehearsal purposes, but note that the lyrics may not match our hymnal. Please practice the lyrics in the Gather hymnal, regardless of the video.
